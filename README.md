# ProperTabGroups for Visual Studio

## Overview

**ProperTabGroups** is an innovative Visual Studio extension designed to revolutionize the way developers organize and interact with their editor tabs. It extends the native tab management capabilities of Visual Studio by introducing customizable, color-coded tab groups with enhanced functionality, including group locking, visibility controls, custom group icons, and drag-and-drop rearrangement. This extension is tailored for developers seeking a more organized, efficient, and personalized coding environment.

## Features

- **Custom Tab Groups**: Easily organize your tabs into customizable groups with custom names.
- **Group Locking**: Lock groups to prevent accidental changes, maintaining your preferred tab organization.
- **Visibility Controls**: Quickly hide or show groups to manage workspace clutter efficiently.
- **Custom Group Icons**: Assign unique icons to each group for quick identification.
- **Drag-and-Drop Rearrangement**: Intuitively rearrange tabs within and across groups to suit your workflow.
- **Color Coding**: Color-code your groups for visual differentiation and faster navigation.
- **Persistence**: Save and load your group configurations to seamlessly continue where you left off.

## Installation

1. Open Visual Studio.
2. Navigate to **Extensions > Manage Extensions**.
3. Search for **ProperTabGroups** in the Online marketplace.
4. Click **Download** and follow the installation prompts.
5. Restart Visual Studio to complete the installation.

## Usage

- **Creating/Managing Groups**: Right-click on a tab to add it to a new or existing group. Groups can be customized through their context menu.
- **Locking/Unlocking Groups**: Click the lock icon in the group header to toggle the lock state.
- **Hiding/Showing Groups**: Use the visibility icon in the group header to collapse or expand groups.
- **Assigning Group Icons**: Through the group's context menu, select "Set Icon" to choose a predefined icon or upload your custom one.
- **Renaming Groups**: Through the group's context menu, select "Rename" to choose a prefered name.
- **Rearranging Tabs**: Drag-and-drop tabs within a group or to another group to rearrange them.
- **Ordered Tabs**: Drag-and-drop tabs will be "Custom Order", other orders will include Alphabetical, Date Opened, etc.
